VOI BLOCKCHAIN DOSSIER

VOI NETWORK is a community-owned Layer 1 blockchain powered by Algorand's groundbreaking Pure Proof of Stake (PPoS) consensus protocol.


▶ 75% COMMUNITY ALLOCATION: Unlike traditional chains dominated by venture capital, Voi allocates 75% of its entire token supply to community members, builders, and node runners.


▶ 2.8 SECOND FINALITY: Transactions achieve instantaneous, mathematical finality in under 3 seconds with zero forks and over 10,000 TPS throughput.


▶ HOW TO PLAY VOI SHOOTER:

  • Control your ship with WASD / Arrow Keys.
  • Aim with the Mouse and hold Left Click or Space to fire.
  • Purge bad code bugs to hit each level's quota (5 on Level 1, 10 on Level 2, 15 on Level 3...).
  • Answer the Voi Blockchain consensus trivia question at the end of each wave.
  • You have 3 Lives total. Protect them! Getting hit or answering trivia wrong loses a life.
  • Survive as many levels and as long as you can to top the Leaderboard!
